03 · KALXA TICKETING

Event Ticketing Terms

KALXA Ticketing provides digital tools that enable event organisers to create, publish and manage events, sell tickets and support QR-based ticket verification.

3.1 Ticketing Commission

KALXA's advertised platform commission is 4% per ticket sold.

The commission is calculated using the applicable ticket selling price, subject to the transaction's confirmed payment and settlement terms.

Example Transaction Amount
Ticket Selling Price R200.00
KALXA Commission (4%) R8.00
Balance Before Other Charges R192.00

The example excludes payment processing fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks and any other applicable transaction adjustments.

The responsible party for payment processing fees and the final settlement calculation must be disclosed through the applicable checkout or organiser agreement.

3.2 Event Organiser Responsibilities

Event organisers are responsible for the accuracy and legality of the events they publish.

  • Providing accurate event dates, locations and descriptions.
  • Displaying correct ticket prices, categories and availability.
  • Obtaining necessary permissions, licences and venue approvals.
  • Managing event operations, safety and access requirements.
  • Communicating event changes and cancellations.
  • Complying with applicable consumer protection laws.
  • Providing accurate business and payment information.

3.3 Ticket Buyers

Ticket buyers should review the event details, organiser identity, ticket price and applicable event conditions before payment.

Digital tickets may contain QR codes or other verification information used to confirm entry eligibility.

Buyers must not intentionally duplicate, manipulate, forge or misuse digital tickets.

3.4 Event Cancellation and Refunds

Event cancellation and refund obligations depend on the applicable transaction, the identity of the seller, the event circumstances and South African law.

Where an independent organiser is the seller of the event ticket, the organiser may be responsible for the event and applicable refund obligations.

KALXA remains responsible for any obligations it has expressly undertaken or that apply to it by law.

Additional information is available in our Refund Policy .
