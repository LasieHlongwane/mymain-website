CREATOR MONETISATION

More Ways to Support Your Creative Work

KALXA Creator is being developed to support different ways for creators to earn from their audiences.

Buy Me a Coffee

Supporters will be able to make voluntary contributions to creators through a planned payment integration. Intended for all creator plans, with commission rates determined by the plan.

Donations & Fundraising

Planned donation and fundraising tools for eligible paid plans. These features are separate from Buy Me a Coffee contributions.

Exclusive Paid Content

Planned tools for offering subscriber-only stories, videos and other premium creative content.

Personal Creator Domain

Premium creators are expected to receive custom domain support, subject to technical availability and applicable domain costs.

Important information:

KALXA Creator is under active development. Some monetisation features described on this page are planned and may not yet be available to users.

Free creators are intended to access Buy Me a Coffee contributions at a 23% KALXA platform commission. Standard and Premium creators are intended to pay a 3% KALXA platform commission on eligible supporter payments.

Standard activation costs R100 once-off, while Premium activation costs R150 once-off. An existing Standard creator may upgrade for an additional R50 once-off after successful payment verification.

Payment functionality is subject to payment provider approval, integration capabilities and applicable fees. KALXA does not guarantee any particular income, donation amount or audience growth.
