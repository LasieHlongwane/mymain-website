PAYMENT & BILLING

Understand Your Payments

Clear information about charges, subscriptions and digital delivery before you purchase.

Once-Off Payments

Kalxa Discovery advertising packages are purchased for a fixed 30-day period and do not renew automatically.

Monthly Subscriptions

Kalxa Ticketing organiser plans, restaurant business profiles and Kalxa Blog Creator CRM are priced monthly. Renewal and cancellation conditions are provided before activation.

Third-Party Transactions

Event tickets, creator memberships and fan contributions may involve independent organisers or creators. The applicable checkout or agreement identifies the seller, recipient, fees and settlement arrangements.

Important payment information: All prices are stated in South African rand (ZAR). Any applicable taxes, transaction fees or additional charges must be disclosed before payment. Payment methods vary by product. The package buttons on this page open enquiries, not a payment checkout.
