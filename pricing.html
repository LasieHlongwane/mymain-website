PAYMENT & BILLING

Understand Your Payments

Different KALXA products use different pricing models. Review the applicable charges and terms before purchasing.

Commission-Based Payments

Event ticketing carries a 4% KALXA platform commission per ticket sold. Creator monetisation has proposed commissions of 23% on Free and 3% on paid plans. Processing fees and other applicable charges are separate unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Once-Off Payments

Creator Standard and Premium use once-off activation fees. Discovery advertising packages are also purchased once for a fixed 30-day period. Optional event promotions are priced separately.

Monthly Subscriptions

Restaurant Standard and Restaurant Premium are monthly subscriptions. Creator activation fees and the 4% event ticketing commission are not monthly subscriptions.

Third-Party Transactions

Event ticket purchases and creator supporter contributions may involve independent organisers or creators. The applicable checkout or agreement should identify the relevant parties, fees and settlement arrangements.

Payment Processing Fees

Payment providers may charge additional processing fees. KALXA's advertised commission percentages do not automatically include those fees. Actual payouts depend on the agreed settlement arrangement and applicable deductions.

Digital Service Delivery

Digital platform access, subscriptions and promotional placements are delivered according to the applicable product terms. Feature availability may depend on activation, verification or technical readiness.
