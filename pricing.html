Pricing & Payments

How KALXA (PTY) LTD prices its digital services and explains payment terms.

PRICING APPROACH

Clear quotations before payment

KALXA (PTY) LTD provides digital services with prices that may vary by product, package, scope or duration. Where a fixed price is not displayed, customers can request a written quotation before placing an order.

Business advertising & promotions

Quoted according to advertising placement, campaign duration, coverage and agreed deliverables.

Platform subscriptions

Applicable subscription price, billing frequency and included features are confirmed before activation.

Digital setup & software services

Setup, configuration and software-related services are quoted according to the agreed scope of work.

PAYMENTS TO KALXA

What KALXA charges for

KALXA may charge for its own digital advertising, platform subscriptions, software services and agreed setup or configuration work. The applicable scope, amount, billing frequency and delivery terms are confirmed before payment.

Some KALXA products may feature services offered by independent event organisers or creators. The relevant checkout or agreement should identify the seller, payment recipient, applicable fees and terms. Payment arrangements for those transactions depend on the product and payment provider.

TICKETING & CREATOR TRANSACTIONS

Third-party payment arrangements

Event ticket prices, creator memberships and fan contributions may involve independent sellers. Any supported checkout should identify the seller, total price, relevant fees and payment terms. Such transactions are not automatically payments to KALXA (PTY) LTD.

PAYMENT & BILLING

Before you complete an order

Customers are informed of the applicable price, whether payment is once-off or recurring, and how digital delivery will take place. Payment methods depend on the product or accepted quotation. This page is informational and is not itself a payment request.
