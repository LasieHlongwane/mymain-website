PAYMENT & BILLING

Understand Your Payments

KALXA provides clear information about applicable charges, subscription arrangements and service delivery.

Once-Off Payments

Kalxa Discovery advertising packages are purchased for a fixed 30-day period. These advertising purchases do not automatically renew.

Monthly Subscriptions

Kalxa Ticketing and Kalxa Blog Creator CRM use monthly subscription pricing. The applicable renewal, payment and cancellation terms are provided before activation.

Third-Party Transactions

Event ticket purchases, fan contributions and creator memberships may involve independent organisers or creators. The relevant payment arrangement determines the recipient, fees and settlement terms.

Important Payment Information: The prices displayed on this page are in South African rand (ZAR). Any applicable taxes, transaction fees or additional charges must be disclosed before payment. Payment methods vary by product and availability. Selecting a package on this page opens an enquiry route rather than charging your account.
