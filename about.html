WHO WE ARE

KALXA (PTY) LTD

KALXA (PTY) LTD is a registered South African private company developing digital technology and platform services. We focus on making it easier for people to discover local opportunities and for businesses and creators to reach and engage their audiences.

Our work spans local digital discovery, business promotion, event ticketing, restaurant profiles and creator-focused publishing tools. We also explore business automation solutions as part of our longer-term product development.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission, Vision & Approach

Our Mission

To develop accessible digital tools that solve practical problems and help businesses, creators and communities participate in the digital economy.

Our Vision

To build a connected ecosystem of useful digital platforms that support local opportunity, business growth and meaningful engagement.

Our Approach

Start with real-world needs, build focused products and improve them through feedback and responsible use of technology.
