Need to know more?

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers to help you find the right part of KALXA.

What is KALXA?

KALXA is a digital technology and platform ecosystem connecting local business discovery, business profiles, event ticketing, creator tools and local opportunities.

How can I list my business?

Visit the KALXA business platform to explore the available business profile options and follow the registration or profile-claiming instructions provided there.

Can I use KALXA to promote an event?

Kalxa Ticketing provides event-related tools. Visit the ticketing platform to explore the available event registration and promotion options.

What is Kalxa Discovery?

Kalxa Discovery helps residents explore local services and temporary work opportunities. The availability of work and earnings is not guaranteed.

Can KALXA build a custom website or application?

KALXA offers custom development services. Contact us with your requirements so we can discuss your project.

How can I contact KALXA?

Visit our contact page, email lasie@kalxa.co.za or use the WhatsApp contact option on this website.
